Sign up and register your organization to get started with Opal, hosted on our secure cloud.

<Warning>
  To set up Opal self-hosted, [use the self-hosted guide](/docs/self-host-overview).
</Warning>

## Set up Opal Cloud

1. Go to [app.opal.dev](https://app.opal.dev).
2. Select **Sign up your org**.
3. Follow the onboarding steps. You'll need to specify your organization name and email.

![](/images/docs/4723379-Opal_Login_Screen.png "Opal Login Screen.png")

After you create your organization, follow the guide to [connect your identity or HR provider](/docs/connect-your-identity-provider-idp).
