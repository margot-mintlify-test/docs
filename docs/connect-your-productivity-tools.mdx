Opal integrates with productivity tools to help your end users access Opal with tools they already use:

1. Communication tools, such as [Slack](/docs/5526194-slack), to streamline notifications and approvals
2. Ticketing providers, such as [Jira](/docs/5526199-jira) and [Linear](/docs/5662665-linear), to integrate [ticket lifecycle](/docs/ticket-propagation-1) with access requests
3. On-call providers, such as [PagerDuty](/docs/5526196-pagerduty) and [Opsgenie](/docs/5526200-opsgenie), to automate break-glass access

To set up a productivity integration:

1. In the Opal dashboard, go to **Configuration > Settings**.
2. Select **Productivity Integrations**.
3. Click **Connect** next to your desired integration.

See the provder-specific guides in this section for more detail on prerequisites and required fields.
